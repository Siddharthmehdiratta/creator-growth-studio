# Platform rules and facts (checked 18 November 2026)

Every skill in this pack uses these. If today is much later than the date above, search for updates before relying on them, and say which ones you could not re-check.

## Instagram
- Ranking: Instagram's head Adam Mosseri named **watch time, sends per reach and likes per reach** as the signals that matter most (January 2025, repeated since). Sends (DM shares) matter most for reaching non-followers. Source: https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/
- **Original content** is ranked above reposts; aggregator accounts get less reach. Same source.
- **Hashtags: 5 per post or Reel at most** since December 2025. Use 3-5 specific ones. Source: https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19
- **Trial reels** are shown to non-followers first and stay off the grid until shared. Use them to test ideas. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers
- **Google indexing**: since 10 July 2025, public posts from professional accounts (18+) can appear in Google search. Captions and alt text matter for search. Source: https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/
- **What Instagram shows the creator**: follower age, gender, top 45 cities and countries, only with 100+ followers. Never income, never who each follower is. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/api-reference/instagram-user/insights
- Per post, Insights include reach, views, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows and profile visits (professional accounts).
- **Other accounts**: only public numbers (followers, posts, likes, comments) are visible. Scraping or automating follows and likes breaks Instagram's terms and risks the account. This pack never does it.
- **Posting automatically**: the official Content Publishing API allows 100 API-published posts per 24 hours for professional accounts, through a Meta developer app. For most creators the free Meta Business Suite planner is the right tool. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/content-publishing

## YouTube
- Partner Program: 500 subscribers (fan funding); 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (ads). **From 1 February 2027**, new channels need 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views. Source: https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/amp/
- YouTube Studio Analytics gives age, gender, geography, traffic sources, retention, impressions click-through rate and subscribers gained per video.

## Twitch
- Affiliate: 25 followers, 4 hours streamed, 4 broadcast days and 3 average viewers within 30 days (lowered from 50 followers and 7 days). Source: https://streamer.guide/guides/twitch-affiliate-requirements

## Ads and disclosure (India and US)
- India's **ASCI** requires a clear, upfront ad label on paid influencer content; brands share responsibility. A platform's own "paid partnership" tag alone may not be enough. In ASCI's February 2025 study, 69% of top digital stars broke the rules. Source: https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Press-Release-Influencer-Disclosure-Guidelines-ASCI-Report.pdf
- Since April 2025, health and finance influencers must show relevant qualifications (finance: SEBI registration for advice). Source: https://www.campaignindia.in/article/influencers-credentials-please-asci-draws-the-line-on-advice/502204
- US: the FTC requires clear disclosure of material connections.

## Benchmarks (ranges, not targets)
- Instagram engagement rate (likes + comments / followers) falls as accounts grow: nano 1K-10K around 4-4.5% median, micro 10K-100K about 3.4% (Buffer, 27M posts, 2026). Other studies report lower numbers. Treat as a range. Source: https://iqfluence.io/public/blog/engagement-rate-instagram
- Spending power (India, for income estimates): urban Indians spend about ₹6,996 per person a month, rural about ₹4,122 (MoSPI HCES 2023-24). Source: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2088390
