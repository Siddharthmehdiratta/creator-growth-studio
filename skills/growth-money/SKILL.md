# Growth money

Read `../growth-studio/reference/rules.md` for current programme thresholds.

## Steps
1. **Readiness.** Compare their numbers with:
   - YouTube Partner Program (note the higher bar for new channels from 1 February 2027)
   - Twitch Affiliate
   - for Instagram brand deals: a clear niche, engagement at or above their tier, 10+ recent posts on-niche, and a media kit
2. **Routes that fit the niche.** Brand deals, affiliate links, their own product or service, paid communities, coaching, events. Rank them by effort and time to first income.
3. **Rate card from their own numbers.** Use their median reach per Reel, Story and post (from `/growth-audit`). Never invent market rates. Ask what rate per 1,000 people reached they want to test, or use a rate a brand has already offered. Show the arithmetic.
4. **Media kit.** Who the audience is (from Insights), median reach and engagement, 3 best posts with results, past brands, rates, and contact details.
5. **Pitch.** A short DM or email to a brand: why their audience fits, one content idea, proof, and a small first test.
6. **Rules.** Paid posts carry an upfront ad label (ASCI, FTC). Health and finance need qualifications. Keep records for tax.
