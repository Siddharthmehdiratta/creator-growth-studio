# Growth SEO

## Instagram
1. **Name field**: Name, a separator, then a keyword people search for. Example: "Rohan | GTA 6 tips". It is searchable inside Instagram.
2. **Username**: easy to say and spell. Add a keyword only if it stays readable.
3. **Bio**: say the topic in words people would type.
4. **Captions**: put the main keyword in the first line. Public professional accounts (18+) can appear in Google results since July 2025, so write captions that answer a search.
5. **Alt text**: describe the image with the keyword, honestly.
6. **Hashtags**: at most 5. They are labels, not reach hacks.
7. **Settings**: keep "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results" on (Settings, then Privacy) for a public professional account.

## YouTube
- Titles promise one outcome. Put the keyword early and keep it under about 60 characters.
- The first 2 lines of the description restate it.
- Use chapters, a pinned comment, and playlists by topic.
- Shorts link to a related long video.

## Twitch
- Titles say what's happening today.
- Choose categories where small streams can be seen.
- Use clips with keyword titles as Shorts or Reels.

## Output
A before-and-after table for the name field, bio, the last 5 captions and the title formats, plus a keyword list grouped by intent (learn, compare, buy, watch).
