2 · Every post, rated

A star rating for all 40 posts

Stars come from the signals Instagram's head names as most important: watch time, sends and likes per person reached, plus follows, saves and comments.

How to read it
  • 3.5 stars is a normal post for you. 5 stars is about twice as good as usual. 1 star is well below.
  • Sort by Best first to find what to repeat, and Worst first to find what to stop.
  • Filter by format to compare reels with reels and carousels with carousels.

Creator Growth Studio3 · Why it worked

3 · Why it worked

Open any post to see why

The best post, "GTA 6 price in India", reached 5,758 people: 4.2 times the usual. Each number sits next to the account's normal, so the reason jumps out.

What the numbers say
  • Profile visits 13x and views 5x: the topic pulled new people in.
  • Sends 3.1x: people sent it to friends, one of Instagram's top 3 signals.
  • Watch time 1.1x: about normal, so the hook, not the length, did the work.
Next time
  • Make a part 2 while interest is fresh.
  • Keep the face-to-camera opening.
  • End with "send this to the friend who's saving up".

A goal you can reach, and the calendar to get there
