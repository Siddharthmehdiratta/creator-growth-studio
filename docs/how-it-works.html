The 3 steps

Pick, bring, read. That's all.

  1. 1

    Pick your platform

    Instagram, YouTube or Twitch. The next step changes to fit it.

  2. 2

    Bring your numbers

    Type a username, connect, upload an export or type in your streams.

  3. 3

    Read your report

    Stars for every post, why each worked, what to post next and a 30-day plan.

How the stars are worked out

Each post gets a score where 100 means a normal post for you.

  1. Per person reached. A big post doesn't win just for being big.
  2. Compared with you. Not with other accounts.
  3. Weighted by what matters. Watch time and sends count most.
  4. Turned into stars. 3.5 is your normal. 5 is about twice as good.
SignalWeight
Average watch time30%
Sends per 1,000 reached30%
Follows per 1,000 reached25%
Likes per 1,000 reached15%
Saves per 1,000 reached15%
Comments per 1,000 reached15%
Hold rate (didn't skip)15%

When a signal is missing, its weight is shared out again.

How patterns are found

Simple averages fool people. If your night posts were all GTA 6 posts, night looks great even when the topic did the work. So the report compares like with like.

  • Format, time, day, hook, topic, length and hashtags are tested together
  • A time trend is included, so growth doesn't make recent habits look magic
  • Only findings that pass a strict test are shown, labelled strong or good evidence
  • On pure random data, fewer than 1 in 10 runs shows a false pattern
  • Groups with fewer than 3 posts are marked thin and never shown as rules

Where your data goes

Short answer: nowhere.

Worked out in your browser

The report is calculated on your own device. Uploaded files never leave it.

Official API only

Instagram numbers come through Meta's official API. No scraping, no bots, no passwords.

Nothing stored

The connector keeps nothing. Your login token stays in your browser tab until you close it.
