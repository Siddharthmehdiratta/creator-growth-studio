Your safety

Why we never ask for your Instagram password

Typing your Instagram password into another website is risky: whoever runs that site could take over your account. A real tool should never need it.

"Log in with Instagram" is the safe way: you type your password on Instagram's own page, Instagram asks you what to share, and we only receive a key to read your numbers. You can remove it any time in Instagram's settings.

Official API only

No scraping, no bots.

Read-only

We can read numbers. We can't post, delete or message.

Files stay on your device

Uploads are read by your browser and never sent anywhere.
