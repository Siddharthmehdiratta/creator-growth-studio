19 questions about your name, bio, posts and habits. You get a score out of 10 and the one thing to fix first. Each rule links to where Instagram said it, and the ones that are only good practice say so.

First look

  • Is it a professional account (Creator or Business)?

    You need one for Insights and trial reels, and for your posts to show on Google. Source

  • Does your name field say what you post about?

    Search matches on text. The name field is one of the places it looks. Source

  • Does your bio say who it is for and what they get?

    Instagram says to put keywords about who you are and what the profile is about in the bio. Source

  • Does your bio ask for one thing only?

    A visitor with one clear next step is more likely to take it than a visitor with five. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Does your link go somewhere useful?

    The link is the only way off Instagram to something you own. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Are your 3 best posts pinned?

    The top row is the first thing a new visitor sees. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Do you have highlights that answer common questions?

    They answer a visitor before they leave to ask. (Good practice. No published rule.)

Your posts

  • Do about 8 in 10 posts serve the same audience?

    Instagram recommends posts to people who liked similar ones. A clear topic makes that match easier. Source

  • Does the first second show movement, a face or a bold line?

    Watch time is the signal Instagram's head names first. Source

  • Do your videos have text on screen or subtitles?

    Many people watch with the sound off. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Is everything you post your own?

    Instagram ranks original content above reposts. Source

  • Would someone send your posts to a friend?

    Sends per person reached is a top signal for reaching people who do not follow you. Source

  • Do you use 5 hashtags or fewer?

    Instagram caps hashtags at 5 per post. Source

  • Do your captions use the words people search for?

    Search reads captions, and public professional posts can show on Google. Source

Your habits

  • Do you post 3 or more times most weeks?

    In Buffer's study of 2 million posts, 3 to 5 a week more than doubled follower growth against 1 to 2. Source

  • Do you reply to comments in the first hour?

    Replies keep a conversation going while the post is still being shown. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Do you post stories most days?

    People who watch your stories see more of your posts. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Do you do a collab post at least once a month?

    A collab post shows on both accounts, so a new audience sees you. (Good practice. No published rule.)

  • Do you test new ideas with trial reels?

    A trial reel goes to people who do not follow you first, so a bad test does not reach your followers. Source
