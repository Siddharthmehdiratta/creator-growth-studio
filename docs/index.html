Questions

Good to know

Still unsure? Read the short guide.

Can I just type my Instagram link?

Yes. Type a username or paste a profile link, and the tool reads that account's public numbers through Instagram's official API: followers, likes, comments and Reel views for the last 50 posts. Reach, watch time, sends and saves are private, so Instagram only shares them after the owner presses Connect Instagram and logs in.

Why doesn't my account show up?

Instagram only lets apps read Creator and Business accounts. If yours is a personal account, switch it in the Instagram app: Settings, then Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account. It's free, takes a minute, and you can switch back.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Your report is worked out inside your own browser. Username lookups and Connect Instagram pass through a small connector that asks Instagram for the numbers and stores nothing. Your login token stays in your browser tab and is gone when you close it. Uploaded files never leave your browser at all.

Does it work with a small account?

Yes. Ratings compare each post with your own typical post, so they work from your first few posts. With fewer than 15 posts, the report says its patterns are early hints, not proof.

How are the stars worked out?

Each post gets a score where 100 is your typical post. It blends reach with quality signals per person reached: watch time and sends count most, then follows, then likes, saves, comments and hold rate. 3.5 stars is a typical post; 5 stars is twice as good as usual.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, and you can read every line on GitHub.
