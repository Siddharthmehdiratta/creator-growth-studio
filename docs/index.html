Questions

Good to know

More answers on the FAQ page, or ask your own question.

Is it really free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence, and you can read every line on GitHub. There's nothing to buy and no account to create.

Do I need to give my password?

No. Typing a username reads public numbers. Connect Instagram uses Instagram's own login page, so the tool never sees your password.

Does it work with a small account?

Yes. Ratings compare each post with your own typical post, so they work from your first few posts. With fewer than 15 posts, the report says its patterns are early hints, not proof.

Which accounts can it read?

Instagram Creator and Business accounts. Switching from a personal account is free and takes a minute in the Instagram app. YouTube and Twitch work from your own numbers.

Ready to see your own report?

Pick Instagram, YouTube or Twitch. It takes under a minute and it's free.
