      30-day plan

      A calendar built from what works for you. Tap a day to see the hook, caption idea and hashtags. Download it to Google Calendar or any calendar app.

      To post automatically, open Meta Business Suite (Planner) or YouTube Studio and schedule each item. They're free and official. Never give your password to a growth app.

      AI boardroom

      Your advisers and an AI audience built from your real followers. They judge your account or your next post, disagree out loud, and the CEO makes the call.
