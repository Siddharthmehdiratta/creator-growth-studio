What do you want to grow?

Pick one. Your report fits the platform, and you can switch any time.

Bring your Instagram numbers

Type your username for a quick report, or connect for the full picture.

@

Best: Meta Business Suite (has reach, views and saves)

  1. On a computer, open Meta Business Suite, then Insights, then Content.
  2. Pick the last 90 days.
  3. Press Export data, then CSV, and drop the file here.

Or: Instagram's own download (ZIP file)

  1. In the Instagram app: your profile, then the menu (top right), then Accounts Center.
  2. Your information and permissions, then Download your information (or Export your information).
  3. Choose your Instagram account, Posts and Reels, format JSON, then Create files.
  4. Instagram emails you when it's ready (minutes to hours). Download the ZIP and drop it here. Nothing leaves your device.

Bring your YouTube numbers

Download one file from YouTube Studio. It takes about 2 minutes.

  1. Open YouTube Studio on a computer, then Analytics, then Advanced mode (top right).
  2. Pick the last 90 days, press the download icon, then Comma-separated values.
  3. Unzip it and drop Table data.csv above.

Add your last streams

Type in at least 3 recent streams from your Creator Dashboard, under Insights. The more you add, the better the patterns.
