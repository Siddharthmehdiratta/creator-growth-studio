Everything your report tells you

One free report that does the work of a growth team. It rates every post, explains why, tells you what to post next and plans your month.

Star ratings

Every post, rated against your own usual

One to five stars from the signals Instagram says matter most: watch time, sends and likes per person reached, plus follows, saves and comments.

  • 3.5 stars is a normal post for you
  • 5 stars is about twice as good as usual
  • Sort by best, worst, newest or reach, and filter by format
sample report

Why it worked

Know exactly what made a post work

Open any post to see reach, views, watch time, skips, sends, saves, follows and comments next to your usual, then a plain reason and what to do next time.

  • Patterns compare like with like, so luck isn't sold as a rule
  • Every finding says how strong the evidence is
sample report

What to post next

Ideas built from your own best posts

Part 2s of your winners, posts built to be sent, and angles from your niche, each with the format, day and time that work for your audience.

  • Trial reels to test new ideas safely
  • A do-more and do-less list from your own numbers
sample report

30-day plan

A goal you can reach, and a calendar to get there

Pick a follower goal. The report says if it's realistic at your pace, then builds a posting calendar on your best days that drops into Google Calendar.

  • Realistic, ambitious or very hard, from your real follows per post
  • Each day has a hook, an angle and up to 5 hashtags
sample report

AI boardroom

Six expert advisers review your account

One message from your report gets Claude to act as a CEO, growth lead, social media expert, SEO lead, data analyst and creative director. They disagree out loud, then the CEO decides.

  • Works on the free Claude plan
  • Includes an AI audience that reacts like real viewers
sample report

Comments sorted

Questions, requests, praise, criticism and spam, in English and Hinglish. Requests become free post ideas.

Audience health check

What a brand sees before paying: engagement against accounts of the same size, and patterns that suggest bought likes.

Three platforms

Instagram, YouTube (videos and Shorts rated separately) and Twitch (streams typed in by hand).
