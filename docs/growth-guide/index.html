How to grow on Instagram, YouTube and Twitch

What the platforms and the biggest studies actually say, turned into steps. Every fact has a source you can check. Updated 19 November 2026.

Instagram

What the algorithm rewards

Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, has named three signals as the most important for reach: watch time, sends per reach (how often people send your post to a friend) and likes per reach.Buffer

Do this

  • Make the first second earn the second: movement, a face, or the result first.
  • Give people a reason to send it. "Send this to the friend who…" works because it names the friend.
  • Post original work. Instagram ranks original content above reposts.Buffer

How often to post

Buffer studied 2 million Instagram posts. Posting 3 to 5 times a week more than doubled follower growth compared with 1 to 2 times, and gave about 12% more reach per post.Buffer

Do this

  • Pick a number you can keep for 3 months. Consistency beats a burst then silence.
  • Use the 30-day plan in your report: it puts posts on your best days and times.

Hooks and length

Reels can be up to 3 minutes long since January 2025.Business Today Longer is allowed, not required: length should follow the story.

Do this

  • Check your report's Reels by length result. It shows which length holds your audience.
  • If the skip rate is high, the opening is the problem.
  • Test one hook style at a time: face to camera, result first, or a question.

Reaching new people

Trial reels are shown only to people who don't follow you, and stay off your grid until you choose to share them.TechCrunch Instagram also caps hashtags at 5 per post since December 2025.Business Today

Do this

  • Test risky ideas as trial reels first. Keep the winners.
  • Use 3 to 5 specific hashtags that describe the post.
  • Collab with creators your size: the post shows to both audiences.

Profile and search

Since 10 July 2025, public posts from professional accounts can appear in Google search.Metricool

Do this

  • Put your main keyword in your name field, for example "Rohan | GTA and BGMI tips".
  • Write captions that say what the post is about in plain words.
  • Pin your 3 best posts so new visitors see why to follow.

YouTube

Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long since 15 October 2024.YouTube blog Test and Compare lets creators test up to three thumbnails, and now titles too, and picks the winner by watch time.Tubefilter

Do this

  • Use Shorts to be found and long videos to be watched. Your report rates them separately.
  • Watch average view duration more than views.

To earn from ads in the YouTube Partner Program, you need 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours in a year, or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days. Fan funding opens at 500 subscribers.Tubefilter

Twitch

Twitch Affiliate needs 25 followers, 4 hours streamed, 4 broadcast days and 3 average viewers within 30 days. Check Twitch Help for the latest.Streamer Guide

Do this

  • Stream on a fixed schedule so people know when to come back.
  • Clip your best moments and post them as Reels and Shorts.
  • Use your report to see which days, start times and lengths bring the most follows.

₹Brand deals and money

In India, ASCI requires a clear, upfront ad label on paid influencer posts, and brands share responsibility. In its February 2025 study, 69% of top digital stars broke the rules.ASCI

Do this

  • Label every paid post at the start: "Ad" or "Paid partnership".
  • Run the audience health check before pitching. Brands look at the same numbers.
  • Price from your real reach, not your follower count.

30Your first 30 days

  1. Week 1Get your report. Write down your 2 best formats and your best posting time.
  2. Week 2Post 3 to 5 times. Make a part 2 of your best post. Test one new hook as a trial reel.
  3. Week 3Reply to every comment in the first hour. Turn the top question into a post.
  4. Week 4Run your report again. Keep what moved up, drop what moved down, change one thing at a time.

No tool can guarantee followers. Anyone who promises that is selling bots, which Instagram removes. Growth comes from posting what your audience sends to friends, often enough to learn what that is.
