1. The CEO's verdict

  • The idea is good. Face to camera, a GTA 6 hashtag and a launch-week topic are the strongest reach patterns in your data.
  • The claim is the problem. "A normal PS5 matters more than the Pro" is a big promise. If your side-by-side does not show it, people will call it out and you lose trust.
  • Do not post it until you have tested it yourself. Then post at night, with subtitles.

7. Honest limits

  • The audience panel is made up. It is a thinking tool, not data. Ten viewers cannot predict real behavior.
  • Your numbers come from 40 posts in a sample export. Groups under 3 posts are hints only.
  • I cannot see the video, the real cover or the real watch time. Watch time is the signal Instagram stresses most, and your export does not include it.
  • I do not know what GTA 6's settings menu offers or whether those 3 settings matter more than a PS5 Pro. That is your claim and it must be tested. I cannot check it.
  • I cannot say how crowded launch week will be. Many creators will post GTA 6 at once.
  • Income is not shown by Instagram. My view of spending power is an estimate from age and city. The reference is about ₹6,996 per person a month for urban India (MoSPI HCES 2023-24). That is an average, not your audience.
  • Reach multipliers are "all else equal" patterns, not promises.
