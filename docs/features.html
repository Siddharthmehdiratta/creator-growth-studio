A star rating for every post

One to five stars, based on the signals Instagram says matter most: watch time, sends and likes per person reached, plus follows, saves and comments.

  • 3.5 stars is a normal post for you
  • 5 stars means about twice as good as usual
  • Sort by best, worst, newest or most reach, and filter by format

Know what to post next

Ideas built from your own best posts, your niche (read from your captions) and proven hook angles, each with the format, day and time that work for your audience.

  • Part 2s of your winners while interest is fresh
  • Trial reels to test new angles without hurting your grid
  • A "do more" and "do less" list from your own numbers

A growth goal and a 30-day plan

Pick a goal from a dropdown. The report says whether it's realistic at your current pace, then builds a posting calendar on your best days that downloads to Google Calendar.

  • Realistic, ambitious or very hard, worked out from your real follows per post
  • Each day has a hook, an angle, a closing line and up to 5 hashtags

Your comments, sorted

When you connect Instagram, comments are read automatically. You can also paste comments from any post. They're sorted so you know what to answer and what to make next.

  • Questions and requests become free post ideas
  • Works in English and Hinglish
  • Spam is flagged so you can hide it

An AI boardroom in Claude

One message, copied from your report, gets Claude to review your account like a full team: a CEO, a growth lead, a social media expert, an SEO lead, a data analyst and a creative director, plus an AI audience that reacts like real viewers.

  • Advisers disagree out loud, then the CEO decides
  • Works on the free Claude plan
