What happens to your data

Short version: your report is worked out on your own device, nothing is stored, and nothing is sold. Updated 19 November 2026.

Files you upload

Exports from Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio or your typed Twitch streams are read by your browser. They never leave your device.

Username lookups

When you type a username, a small connector asks Instagram's official API for that account's public numbers and passes them straight back. It keeps no copy and no log of who asked.

Connect Instagram

You log in on Instagram's own page. The tool never sees your password. Instagram gives your browser a temporary key, which stays in that browser tab and is gone when you close it.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no database and no advertising tracker. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may keep standard server logs under GitHub's own privacy policy.

The AI boardroom

It works by copying a message into your own Claude app. What you paste there is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy, not ours.
