What's new

Every change to Creator Growth Studio, newest first. The full history is on GitHub.

  1. 19 Nov 2026

    A new website

    • A proper home page with real screenshots of the report
    • Separate pages for features, pricing, help, contact, about and privacy
    • Report a problem and give feedback straight from the contact page

  2. 18 Nov 2026

    YouTube and Twitch

    • Pick your platform first: Instagram, YouTube or Twitch
    • YouTube Studio exports, with Shorts and long videos rated separately
    • Twitch streams typed in by hand, rated on viewer-hours, follows and chat

  3. 18 Nov 2026

    Type a username, or connect Instagram

    • Read any public Creator or Business account by username
    • Connect your own account for reach, watch time, skips, sends and comments

  4. 19 Nov 2026

    Ratings out of 10 that learn

    • Every post rated out of 10 against your own usual post
    • The Post Checker learns from your report: format, length, day, time and hashtags
    • A caption score out of 10
    • Growth Plan: 8 questions, a weekly pattern, and why
    • Ads Checker: is your ad paying for itself?

  5. 18 Nov 2026

    First release

    • Star ratings for every post, why each worked, what to post next and a 30-day plan
    • An AI boardroom of six advisers in Claude
    • An audience health check for brands
