Your pattern for more followers

3 posts a week

2 reels + 1 carousel. In Buffer's study of 2 million posts, 3 to 5 posts a week more than doubled follower growth against 1 to 2. Source: Buffer: 2 million posts

Your week

  1. MonReel19:30
  2. TueRest
  3. WedCarousel19:30
  4. ThuRest
  5. FriReel19:30
  6. SatRest
  7. SunRest

There is no single best time for everyone. Start with this time for 4 weeks, then use your report. It shows which day and time worked for your own posts. The days spread your posts across the week; they are a starting pattern, not an Instagram rule.

What to post
2 × Reel. Most reels people see come from accounts they do not follow. Reels are how new people find you. Source: Instagram: Ranking Explained1 × Carousel. People swipe through and save carousels. Good for teaching and for explaining an offer. Common practice.
The number to move
Sends per reach. Sends count slightly more for reaching people who do not follow you yet. Source: Social Media Today, quoting MosseriMake each post something a person would forward to one friend. Work out sends ÷ accounts reached.
How to open
Open with your face, talking, in the first second. Say the point of the video before anything else. No intro, no logo. Common practice.
How to end
End with a send line. Name the friend. It turns a like into a send. "Send this to a friend who needs to see it."
Your views are low
  1. Check the post is original, sharp, with sound, no watermark from another app and not mostly text. Instagram shows those less.
  2. Rework the first second. If nothing happens, people swipe.
  3. Try a trial reel: it is shown to people who do not follow you first.
  4. Source: TechCrunch: trial reels
What to track
  • Sends ÷ accounts reached
  • Follows per post
  • Reach from people who do not follow you
Your 4-week test
  1. Weeks 1 and 2: post the pattern as written. Change nothing.
  2. Week 3: change one thing only. Start with the last line.
  3. Week 4: put your numbers into the report. Keep what beat your normal. Drop what did not.
Do not do this
  • Watermarks from other apps, reposts of other people's work, low resolution, muted reels, borders, and posts that are mostly text. Instagram shows these less. Source: Instagram: Ranking Explained
  • More than 5 hashtags. Instagram caps them at 5. Source: Business Today: 5-hashtag cap
For your size
Under 1,000 followers: stay on one topic, so Instagram learns who to show you to. Common practice.
