How to use it

10 minutes the first time. No coding, no login. Your numbers stay in your browser.

Instagram (6 steps)

  1. Switch to a Professional account if you haven't: Settings, then Account type and tools. It's free and unlocks Insights.
  2. On a computer, open business.facebook.com (Meta Business Suite) and log in with your Instagram.
  3. Go to Insights, then Content. Pick the last 90 days. Press Export data and choose CSV.
  4. Optional, but it makes the answers much better: open the CSV in Google Sheets or Excel and add 2 columns, Hook (for example Face to camera, Gameplay first, Text on screen) and Topic. Or label posts later in the studio.
  5. Open the studio. Fill in Your account, then drop the CSV into Your numbers.
  6. Read your Diagnosis, check the Growth map, download the 30-day plan to your calendar, and pick up to 3 fixes for this week.

No computer? Take screenshots of Insights on your phone (the Audience tab, and a few posts' insights). Paste them into Claude with the boardroom message and say "read my numbers from these screenshots".

YouTube

  1. Open YouTube Studio, then Analytics, then Advanced mode.
  2. Choose the last 90 days, press the download icon, and pick Comma-separated values.
  3. Unzip it and drop Table data.csv into the studio.

Twitch

  1. Open Creator Dashboard, then Insights, then Stream summary.
  2. In the studio, press Add a post by hand for each stream. Put average viewers times hours streamed in Reach, followers gained in Follows, and clips in Sends.

The AI boardroom (3 steps)

  1. In the studio, open AI boardroom. Pick what to review (for example "A post before I publish it") and paste your draft hook, caption or script.
  2. Choose your advisers and your audience: ages, gender split and where they live, from Insights, then Audience.
  3. Press Copy the message for Claude, open claude.ai or the Claude app, paste, and send. On the free plan, 10 viewers is lighter.

You get a CEO verdict, a scorecard from each adviser, an AI audience table (who stopped, watched, sent, saved, followed and what they'd comment), the advisers' disagreements, 3 actions for this week, and rewrites.

For brands and agencies

Open For brands in the studio. Type a creator's followers and the likes, comments and views of their last 12 posts, and tick which were paid and labelled. You get engagement against their tier, warning signs, and missing ad labels. Then ask the creator for an Insights screenshot before paying.

The full version in Claude Code

If you use Claude Code (a paid Claude plan), paste github.com/Siddharthmehdiratta/creator-growth-studio and say "install this skill pack". Then type /growth-studio in the folder with your export. The boardroom then runs every AI viewer as a separate agent, and you get calendar files and reports saved in your folder.

Honest limits

  • Instagram never shows anyone their followers' income, or exactly who each follower is. The studio estimates and says so.
  • AI advisers and AI audiences are simulations. Use them to find weak spots, then test with trial reels.
  • Patterns in your posts are not proof. Change one thing at a time for 2 weeks, then check again.
  • No tool can guarantee followers. Anyone who promises that is selling bots.
