How to use it

Under a minute the first time. No forms, no coding and no password.

Get your report

  1. Open the studio.
  2. Type your Instagram username, or paste your profile link, and press Get my report.
  3. For the full picture, press Connect Instagram and log in through Instagram's own page. This adds reach, watch time, skip rate, sends, saves, follows and your comments.

Account not found? Instagram only lets apps read Creator and Business accounts. In the Instagram app, open Settings, then Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account. It's free and takes a minute.

Read your report

TabWhat it tells you
OverviewYour average rating, your level, the patterns that stand out, your best and weakest posts, and an audience health check.
PostsEvery post with 1 to 5 stars. Open one to see each number next to your usual, why it worked or didn't, and what to do next time. Your comments are sorted here too.
What to postIdeas built from your best posts and your niche, with the format, day and time that work for you.
PlanWhat your follower goal really takes, and a 30-day calendar you can add to Google Calendar.
AI boardroomA message to paste into Claude. A CEO, growth, social media, SEO, data and creative leads review your account, and an AI audience reacts like real viewers.

3.5 stars is a typical post for you. 5 stars means about twice as good as your usual. Every number compares you with yourself, so the report works at any size.

Use an export file instead

  1. On a computer, open Meta Business Suite, then Insights, then Content.
  2. Pick the last 90 days, press Export data, and choose CSV.
  3. Drop the file onto the studio. For YouTube, use YouTube Studio, then Analytics, then Advanced mode, then download Table data.csv.

For brands and agencies

Type the creator's username. The Overview tab shows an audience health check: engagement against accounts of the same size, comments per like, and patterns that suggest bought likes. Before you pay, ask the creator for an Insights screenshot of their audience's ages and cities. No tool can see those for another account.

Honest limits

  • A username alone shows public numbers only. Reach, watch time, sends and saves need the owner to connect.
  • Patterns in your posts are hints, not proof. Change one thing at a time for 2 weeks, then check again.
  • AI advisers and AI audiences are simulations. Use them to find weak spots, then test with trial reels.
  • No tool can guarantee followers. Anyone who promises that is selling bots.
