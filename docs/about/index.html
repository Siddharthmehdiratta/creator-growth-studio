Growth advice that fits your account, not someone else's

Creator Growth Studio is a free, open-source tool made by Siddharth Mehdiratta in Pune, India. It turns the numbers you already have into a clear, honest plan.

Most growth advice is generic. Your numbers aren't.

Every creator hears the same tips: post more, use trends, hook in the first second. Some of it is true for you, and some of it isn't.

The only way to know is to look at your own posts and compare like with like. That's slow and fiddly by hand, so this tool does it for you, in under a minute, for free.

Four rules the tool never breaks

Honest over impressive

Every pattern says how strong the evidence is. Thin evidence is labelled, and no tool can promise followers.

Your data stays yours

The report is worked out in your browser. No account, no database, no password.

Official data only

Instagram's official API and the exports platforms give you. No scraping and no bots.

Free and open

Every line of code is public under the MIT licence, so anyone can check it, copy it and improve it.
