Your first report in 7 steps

New to all this? Go one step at a time. Each step takes 1 to 3 minutes.

Start nowSee the sample report
1Know what you'll get2Get your account ready3Pick your platform4Bring your numbers5Read your stars6See why each post worked7Act on it, then check again

  1. Step 1 of 7

    Know what you'll get

    A report that tells you which posts grew your account, why, and what to post next. About 10 minutes in total.

    Do this

    1. Have your phone or computer ready.
    2. Know which platform you want to grow: Instagram, YouTube or Twitch.
    3. That's it. No account, no password, no payment.

    Want to look first? Open the sample report. It's a fictional account analysed by the real tool.

  2. Step 2 of 7

    Get your account ready

    Only Instagram needs one setting changed. YouTube and Twitch need nothing.

    Do this

    1. Instagram: open the app, go to Settings, then Account type and tools, then Switch to professional account. Pick Creator. It's free and you can switch back.
    2. YouTube: nothing to do yet.
    3. Twitch: nothing to do yet.

    Instagram only lets tools read Creator and Business accounts. That's Instagram's rule.

  3. Step 3 of 7

    Pick your platform

    Open the tool and choose where you post.

    Do this

    1. Press Get my free report.
    2. Press Instagram, YouTube or Twitch.
    3. The next screen shows only the options for that platform.

  4. Step 4 of 7

    Bring your numbers

    Pick the easiest way for you. Each one is explained on screen.

    Do this

    1. Instagram: type your username, press Connect Instagram, or upload an export from Meta Business Suite.
    2. YouTube: in YouTube Studio open Analytics, then Advanced mode. Pick the last 90 days, press the download icon, choose CSV, and upload the file called Table data.csv.
    3. Twitch: type in your last 5 to 10 streams: date, start time, average viewers, hours, new followers and chat messages.

    Username lookup and Connect Instagram switch on once the site's Meta app is approved. Until then, the export upload gives the same detail.

  5. Step 5 of 7

    Read your stars

    Every post gets 1 to 5 stars, compared with your own typical post.

    Do this

    1. 3.5 stars means a normal post for you.
    2. 5 stars means about twice as good as usual. 1 star means well below.
    3. Sort by Best first to see your top posts, then Worst first.

  6. Step 6 of 7

    See why each post worked

    Open any post. Each number sits next to your usual, with a plain reason.

    Do this

    1. Look for the biggest green number. That's what made it work.
    2. Look for the red one. That's what held it back.
    3. Read the Next time line and write it down.

  7. Step 7 of 7

    Act on it, then check again

    Growth comes from repeating what works and changing one thing at a time.

    Do this

    1. Open What to post and pick one idea.
    2. Open Plan and add the 30-day calendar to Google Calendar.
    3. Post 3 to 5 times a week for 4 weeks.
    4. Run your report again and compare.

  8. You're ready.

    Stuck on any step? The help centre answers it, or you can ask in public.
